What changed and why

  1. Filters collapse behind one row. not built The search bar is four rows deep before a single result appears — lookup, query, five filter controls, then the bands. In a sidebar that is a lot of vertical spend on controls you touch once per search. Keep the query and Search; put type, period, creator, base model and the bands behind a disclosure row that shows how many are active. medium
  2. Active filters as removable chips. shipped With a creator, two base models and two bands set, nothing on screen tells you what is applied — you have to read five separate controls. Chips state it in one line and let you drop one filter without hunting for it. This is also the answer to "why am I only seeing four results?". small
  3. Pin the pager to the bottom of the viewport. shipped It currently sits below the grid, so after scrolling through a page of cards you scroll further to reach it. Sticky positioning keeps Prev/Next under your thumb the whole time. small
  4. Quick actions on the card. shipped Downloading takes a click into the detail modal and then another click. A download and a bookmark button on hover removes that for the common case; the modal stays for everything else. While there, the "✓ have it" badge from the hash check could sit on any model you already own. medium
  5. A one-line results header. not built "7 models by Marlosart · sorted by Highest Rated" — the count and the ordering stay visible instead of living in the footer you have to scroll to. small

Smaller things, each an hour or so

  1. Empty states that suggest something. "No models match" could offer to clear the chips, or widen the bands, rather than just reporting the fact.
  2. Keyboard hint on the search field. /, arrows and ? all work but are invisible until you press ?. A faint / in the corner of the query field is the usual cue.
  3. Sort next to the results count rather than in the search bar — it describes the results, it does not filter them.
  4. Aspect-ratio placeholders are already right (3/4), so nothing shifts as images arrive; the same treatment for the lightbox would stop its own jump.

The band row is drawn at its new full width in both panels — five bands plus All on one line, which is what the label and hint were taking the space from.
